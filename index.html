My story

I was born in Argentina, spent ten years living in Paris, and moved to Barcelona at the end of 2023. Each place has shaped the way I see people, work and change. Moving between countries has taught me to adapt, stay curious and find common ground with people from different backgrounds.

Today, I’m looking to build my next chapter in Barcelona — not just by finding a job, but by becoming part of a team and a place where I can grow over time.

How I work

I enjoy collaborative environments where people communicate openly, share context and work towards a common goal. I’m process- and software-friendly: I like understanding how things work, bringing structure where it helps, and using tools to make work clearer and more effective.

I’m results-driven, and I don’t expect good results to happen by chance. I’m willing to work hard, stay consistent and keep learning when something doesn’t go as planned. Across my experiences, I’ve learned how important clear communication is — especially in a world that moves quickly and where priorities can change. Resilience, for me, means adjusting, learning and continuing towards the goal.

A note on motivation

I’m looking for an opportunity to keep developing professionally in Barcelona, ideally within a multicultural environment where collaboration, learning and growth are part of the culture.

My background has taken me across sales, account management, client relationships and business operations. These experiences have shown me that results come not only from working hard, but also from listening, communicating clearly, understanding the bigger picture and finding practical ways forward.

I bring curiosity, commitment and a willingness to learn. I like having clear goals, taking ownership of my work and contributing to a team that wants to move forward together. I don’t expect to know everything from day one; I value environments where people can ask questions, share knowledge and keep improving.

I’m now looking for a place where I can contribute with what I already know while continuing to build new skills and take on new challenges.

That’s the next chapter I’m hoping to build in Barcelona.
