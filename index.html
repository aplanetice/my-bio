A little about me

My story

After completing my studies in Buenos Aires, I moved to Paris in 2014. France became an important part of my personal and professional journey, giving me the opportunity to work in inspiring, international environments and learn from different people, teams and ways of thinking. In 2024, I arrived in Barcelona, ready to begin a new chapter.

Along the way, I’ve developed a genuine interest in the way technology, creativity and contemporary culture shape how we live and work. I love making new connections, discovering people’s stories and staying open to perspectives that are different from my own.

What inspires me

I enjoy contributing something positive wherever I am — whether that means helping a team move forward, making a process clearer, supporting a client or bringing a fresh perspective to a challenge. I’m curious by nature and genuinely enjoy learning about different subjects, tools and ways of working.

I’m motivated by goals, both professional and personal. I like having something meaningful to work towards, putting in the effort and seeing progress take shape. I value collaboration and clear communication, but I also appreciate the focus and responsibility that come with owning an objective and following it through.

Technology · Art · Contemporary culture · New connections · Continuous learning

What’s next

I’m looking to build my next chapter in Barcelona within an international, collaborative environment where I can contribute my experience, keep learning and grow alongside people who care about what they do.

My professional journey has given me opportunities across client relationships, sales, account management and operations. I bring curiosity, commitment and a practical mindset, along with a real appreciation for teamwork and for the small, thoughtful actions that make a positive difference.

I don’t expect to have every answer from day one. I’m excited by the chance to ask questions, discover new stories, take on challenges and keep moving towards shared goals — with an open mind and a positive attitude.
